# Local rival duel — The 3AM Fridge Text

Judge: the house judge's published brief (competition.yaml), applied by hand.
Criteria, most important first:
1. Is a one-line text a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am, and is funny.
2. Gets the bigger laugh: the line a reader would screenshot and send to a friend.
3. Sounds like a fridge — a machine with a small voice, an opinion, no shame at 3am.
4. Works as one line of a text message, short on a lock screen, complete without follow-up.
5. Fresh angle instead of the obvious one about milk, ice or a door left open.

Scored 1–5 per criterion. Pairwise head-to-heads run in both orders (A/B swap);
the judge's rule: first criterion where they differ decides, unless a later one
differs by much more.
